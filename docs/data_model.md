### Cleaning rules applied

1. **Exact duplicates** — dropped.
2. **Near-duplicates** `(date, amount, counterparty)` — flagged (`is_near_duplicate`), kept.
3. **Dates** — coerced to `datetime64[ns]`; unparseable rows dropped.
4. **Amounts** — coerced to float64; nulls dropped.
5. **Critical nulls** — rows missing `transaction_id`, `date`, `amount`, `category` or `type` dropped.
6. **Currency** — defaulted to EUR; forced uppercase.
7. **Derived columns** — `year_month`, `is_income`, `is_expense`.

### Categories present

Salary, Freelance (income); Rent, Groceries, Dining, Transport, Utilities, Subscriptions, Healthcare, Entertainment, Travel, Shopping, Insurance, Investment (expense).
